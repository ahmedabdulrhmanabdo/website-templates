Featured project

ERPNext as a self-service SaaS

I connected ERPNext to a complete subscription service that runs on its own. A customer signs up, picks a plan and gets a working ERPNext site, with no one on my side touching it.

  1. Customer signs up through a public form.
  2. A separate ERPNext site is created on its own subdomain.
  3. Modules for the chosen plan are switched on.
  4. Trial period, monthly or yearly subscription and user limits are managed automatically.
  • Plans can include invoicing, accounting, inventory, and HR with payroll.
  • The system controls how many users each customer can have.
  • E-invoicing follows ZATCA, Phase 1 and Phase 2.
  • A mobile app for invoices is connected to the system.
